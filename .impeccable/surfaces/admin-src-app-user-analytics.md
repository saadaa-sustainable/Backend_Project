## Scope

The analytics surface of the saadaa admin app:
`admin/src/app/user/analytics` — Ads Analyse, Creative Testing, CPIS,
Untested Assets, Last-Click UTM, Customer Journey, Landing Page
Analysis, Shopify Analytics, Instagram, Meta Explorer.

Visitor mode: **Operate**. Three audiences share it — media buyers
making daily scale/pause calls, founders reading outcomes, and the
creative team deciding what to produce next — at a desk, all day, on a
large monitor, in long sessions.

Preserved by instruction, not by taste: the framework vocabulary
(SCALE / PAUSE / MONITOR / REPORT, F1–F4, CBO/ABO, NCP, FTEWV), table
density, and the InfoDot basis panels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One mark, one unit — the chart grammar built so a number cannot
lie about its own precision. This surface refuses the arrangement every
analytics product ships: a row of rounded KPI cards floating on a grey
page above a boxed table, each number stated without its basis and each
accent colour meaning nothing in particular.

OWN-WORLD: Isotype. Paper `#F2F0EB`, ink `#14120E`, signal red
`#C8102E` reserved by law for a fired rule, Vienna blue `#1D4E89` and
ochre `#D99A00` for data only. Rules, never boxes: hairlines separate
rows and sections, and nothing on the surface carries both a border and
a shadow. Geometric sans throughout, tabular numerals everywhere a
figure appears. Drawn pictograms, never glyphs or emoji. The existing
CVD-validated chart palette survives as the data inks.

STORY: A buyer lands on the account's verdict bands, sees where the rule
fired in red, follows it into the table, opens the basis beside the
number, and acts without leaving the page. A founder reads the top band
and stops. A creative goes straight to the asset sections and never
meets adset mechanics.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Section name against a single full-width rule. Beneath
it the verdict band — SCALE / PAUSE / MONITOR as flat colour fields
carrying counts, spanning the content width, not cards. Beneath that the
table at full bleed to the content column, hairline rules between rows,
no container. Filters sit on one rule above the table as labelled cells,
not floating selects. Signal red appears only where a rule fired.

FORM: The Vienna Method (Isotype, Neurath and Arntz, 1925–34); candidate
6 of my grounded list, dealt by seed key `92e72f3b`. Raised by four
declined hands: total environmental commitment (Leather), disclosure as
structure (Miura), state as one continuous ramp (Cyclorama), one colour
reserved by law (Arcade).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with
the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster
carrying its provenance.
